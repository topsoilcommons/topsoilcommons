# Collaborate with Topsoil Commons

Topsoil Commons builds free, open source tools for the needs every person has, and keeps a public record anyone can check. This page lists concrete ways an organization can work with it. Nothing here implies an existing partnership; collaborations are invitations until they happen, and no partner is named publicly without their written agreement.

## Who this is for

Libraries, schools, food and health providers, community groups, local offices, and anyone who keeps a public record other people rely on.

## Sized tasks

1. **Check one thing.** Pick one claim, one listing, or one entry, and check it against its source. Publish the check under your name. It takes minutes, and it is the whole method in miniature.
2. **Adopt a board or a record.** Fork Ground for your town's help listings, or run an Index for your community's records, under the same rules: primary sources, dated checks, nothing hidden. Tell us, and your fork is linked from the project's page.
3. **Bring a record that needs checking.** If your organization publishes information people act on, and keeping it true is the hard part, that is the work this Commons practices in the open. Start with one board or one list.

## What you get

A public, dated record of the work, with your organization's role stated as you approve it, in repositories anyone can audit.

## Start

Open an issue in this repository. Small and specific beats grand and vague.
