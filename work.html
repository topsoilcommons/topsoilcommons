Verification systems

The Ground board

The Commons' own help board, and the standing sample of the verification work. 158 listings stand checked across 19 places, counted on October 17, 2026, every one read against its provider's own page. Each listing carries the date it was checked and an expiry date 30 days later, and the board runs on a 30 day recheck cycle: a listing that fails its recheck is dropped, and the drop is recorded. Read the board.

check

A standalone tool that does the Ground check for any board. It reads a directory of help listings, checks each listing again against the provider's own page, stamps the listings that still hold with the day of the run and an expiry date 30 days later, and names every failure in plain words. Its own test suite passes 8 of 8 tests.

prove

The standalone validator for a Proof: a claim bound to its ground, its hand, and its check, with its links and its seal. prove recomputes the seal and prints a one page verdict that any stranger can rerun. Its test suite passes 9 of 9 tests.

chain

A hash chain builder and verifier for any chronological record, taken from the Index's own design. It stamps each entry over the entry before it, and its verifier catches an edited entry, a removed entry, and a reordered pair, and prints which check failed. Its test suite passes 8 of 8 tests.

Together, the three suites pass 25 of 25 tests, counted from the runs the progress log records on October 18, 2026. The suite is finished and stands in the public record. Its repository is being published now, and this page will link to it when it stands; until then, nothing here links to a repository that does not exist.

Standing Record

Opening soon. Standing Record is the verification work kept true as a subscription. A board checked once goes stale within a season, so this service rechecks the board every month and keeps its record standing.

What a subscriber receives, every month

Every listing on the board rechecked against the provider's own page. A stamped record page for the board that anyone can read. A report naming each listing that changed, each that failed, and each that was corrected. And corrections filed with the providers and followed until they resolve.

The three sizes

The price is set by the size of the board. Board: $499 a month, for a board of up to 150 listings. Network: $999 a month, for a board of up to 500 listings. Region: $2,499 a month, for a board of up to 2,000 listings, with the corrections work and a review call every three months carried in the price.

Standing Record is opening soon and takes no payment today. To ask about a board, send a message to the Topsoil Commons page on LinkedIn.
