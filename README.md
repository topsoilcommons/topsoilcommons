# Topsoil Commons

Topsoil Commons addresses the hierarchy of human needs through open source.

The map: [The Ascent](NEEDS.md). The method: build on proven open source codebases. The motto: Stand on the shoulders of giants.

One rule: raise the floor beneath everyone.

## How this place runs

Everything in this organization is open source and public: the code in this repository stands under the MIT license, each project carries its own, and the record, the ledger, and the log stand open for anyone to check. Nothing is sold here, and no money is taken here. Gifts to the Commons move only through a fiscal host; none is appointed yet, and [FUNDS.md](FUNDS.md) says so plainly.

The builder earns his living separately, in the open, in his own name: verification work, priced pilots, claims audits, and training, offered at [topsoilcommons.org/services](https://topsoilcommons.org/services). That work is his, not the Commons', and it buys no governance, no votes, and no standing here.

## Early exhibits

- **Ground**: a board that verifies local help listings against each provider's own page; 158 listings across 19 places, every check dated and expiring 30 days later. Layer 0, the body. [Live demo](https://topsoilcommons.github.io/ground/) · [Repository](https://github.com/topsoilcommons/ground)
- **Still**: a quiet single page tool to check in, breathe, and write; one file, zero network calls, nothing leaves the device. Layer 1, the mind at peace. [Live demo](https://topsoilcommons.github.io/still/) · [Repository](https://github.com/topsoilcommons/still)
- **Atlas**: a learning atlas whose lessons cite their sources; its archived tutor eval stands at groundedness 0.922 on 17 questions, every answer citing a retrieved file. Layer 2, learning. [Live demo](https://topsoilcommons.github.io/atlas/) · [Repository](https://github.com/topsoilcommons/atlas)
- **Cohort**: a community platform where people and AI agents share rooms as members; its MCP server joins as a real client, the test suite passes 8 of 8, and a live session transcript is committed as evidence. Layer 3, belonging. [Live demo](https://topsoilcommons.github.io/cohort/) · [Repository](https://github.com/topsoilcommons/cohort)
- **Machine**: machine learning built from scratch in pure Python; a transformer with gradient checks 7 of 7 and a suite of 18 tests, all green. Layer 5, autonomy. [Live demo](https://topsoilcommons.github.io/machine/) · [Repository](https://github.com/topsoilcommons/machine)
- **Graph**: a tool that extracts the entities and relations a text states, and publishes its misses; entities F1 0.8805, relations F1 0.6034 on 22 hand labeled documents. Layer 4, shared truth. [Live demo](https://topsoilcommons.github.io/graph/) · [Repository](https://github.com/topsoilcommons/graph)
- **Lumen**: a guarded question answerer studied over three public domain texts; in each exhibit, 12 of 12 guarded questions refused and 8 of 8 allowed questions answered. Layer 6, trust and meaning. [Live demo](https://topsoilcommons.github.io/lumen/) · [Repository](https://github.com/topsoilcommons/lumen)

## Where this is headed (projections, gated, not promises)

- **Phase 0, Foundation (Oct to Dec 2026):** all five acts published with evaluated baselines. Gate: every claim traces to a logged run.
- **Phase 1, Proof (Jan to Jun 2027):** real use, a pilot for the atlas, a public cohort in the commons, eval sets grown to credible sizes. Gate: outside users in at least two acts.
- **Phase 2, Integration (Jul to Dec 2027):** the acts interoperate; the atlas tutor grounds through the graph and answers under the guardrail policy layer. Gate: two cross project integrations live and verified.
- **Phase 3, Scale (2028):** institutional partnerships. Gate: institutions depending on at least one act in real work.
- **Phase 4, Commons (2029 to 2031):** an open ecosystem with contributors, forks, and shared governance. Gate: the work improves without one person's daily effort.

A phase that misses its gate gets a written reason and a revised projection, never a quiet edit. What counts here is human outcomes, honestly counted and dated. No numbers appear until they are real.

- [Intentions](INTENTIONS.md): what this work is for, what it will never do, and how it earns, stated plainly.

- [Billions, verifiably](PARTICIPATION.md): how the Commons grows to reach everyone. Taking part is counted in tiers nobody can inflate, fraud is published beside the counts, and votes lead to real change. The examples it learns from and the code it can reuse are cited.

- [Prompts](PROMPTS.md): copy paste prompts that let anyone, or any agent, contribute: run an Eval Challenge, file a failure report, take a good first issue, adopt a basic tool, audit a claim.
