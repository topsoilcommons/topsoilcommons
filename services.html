Work you can hire

These offers are the working practice of Joshua Thomas, the builder of Topsoil Commons. The methods are the Commons' own methods, proven in its public record, and the record itself stays free for everyone. What is sold here is the work of doing it for your place, your program, or your team.

Every engagement starts the same way: a written offer before any work begins, naming the scope, one fixed price, and what will stand in the open when the work is done. No hourly meter. To start, send a message to the Topsoil Commons page on LinkedIn saying which offer fits and where your place is.

Verification of a help board

What you get

Your place's food, shelter, health, and safety listings checked one by one against each provider's own page: the address, the hours, and the service confirmed, a checked date and an expiry date on every listing that passes, and every listing that fails named, with the reason, in the open.

The standing sample

Ground, the Commons' own board: 164 listings stand checked across 19 places, counted on October 21, 2026, every one against its provider's own page. Read the board.

Price

One fixed price for the place, set in the written offer. The size of the place sets the size of the price, and the offer says both before you agree to anything.

Price

A claims audit

What you get

Your public claims, on a site, in a report, or in a funding document, checked against their sources one by one. Each claim comes back marked: it stands, it stands with its limits printed, or it does not stand, with the source that settles it named beside the mark.

The standing sample

Foundations: 71 findings stand in the open today, each runnable in under a minute, each with its source named and its limits printed. Run one.

Price

One fixed price for the set of claims, set in the written offer once the set is counted.

Training

Sessions for adults and for teams, in person or remote: working with AI tools honestly, what they can and cannot do, checked the way this site checks everything; and running a program whose records stay straight, listings, counts, and claims that a stranger can verify.

Price

Two kinds of money, kept apart

Paying for work on this page buys the work, and only the work. It is the builder's living, earned in his own name, and it buys no governance, no votes, no standing, and no place in the Commons beyond the credit any verified contribution earns. Giving is a different door entirely: the Commons receives gifts only through a fiscal host, no host is appointed today, and that door is shut until one is. The funds page tells the whole money story in the open, and the giving endpoint answers in the open that the door is shut and why.

One rule: raise the floor beneath everyone.
