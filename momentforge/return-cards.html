For Joshua only — do not hand this strip out.
Print this file (Letter, black and white is fine). Cut out the 5 cards on the solid lines and fold each on the dashed line so the MomentForge side faces out.
Hand one to a person you already know, in person. Fill the front in together in under two minutes: one anchor, the smallest action, when they will leave to do it.
They keep the card. When they come back, they fill the inside in their own words and add a mark from the real world.
Count returns within 7 days on the tally page at the end. A card that never returns is data — record it plainly.
No card for a person in crisis as a substitute for help. Permission before any card is photographed, quoted, or shown.
